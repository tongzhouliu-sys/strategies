03
5 项市场准入门槛
5 Pre-Screen Gates
任一不过即短路，不拉期权链
Any failure short-circuits — no options chain fetch
⚠
设计原则：Gate 执行成本极低（无需拉期权链），任一门槛不通过即立即返回，全量扫描随之中止。VIX 35 是唯一不可软化的绝对熔断阈值，AI 也无权建议放宽。 Design Principle: Gates are nearly zero-cost (no options chain fetch needed). Any failure immediately returns. VIX 35 is the sole absolute circuit-breaker threshold — AI cannot suggest relaxing it.
Gate-1
市场状态
Market Regime
SP500 在 200 日均线下方 且 20 日回撤超 10% → 市场 D 级，直接否决。两者须同时满足才触发，单独低于均线仅警告。
SP500 below 200-day MA AND 20-day drawdown >10% → Market Grade D, rejected. Both conditions must be met; failing MA alone only issues a warning.
Gate-2
VIX 硬上限
VIX Hard Ceiling
VIX > 35 时绝对禁止卖出任何期权。唯一不可软化的熔断阈值，AI 无权建议放宽，无任何例外。
VIX > 35 absolutely prohibits selling any options. The sole non-negotiable circuit-breaker. AI cannot suggest relaxation; no exceptions.
Gate-3
VIX 期限结构（倒挂监控）
VIX Term Structure (Inversion Monitor)
VIX > VIX3M（期限倒挂）时，DELTA_MIN 自动收紧 +0.02，排除最激进 Delta 端。不直接否决，但隐含风险偏好收紧。
VIX > VIX3M (term inversion): DELTA_MIN auto-tightens by +0.02, removing the most aggressive delta range. Not a direct rejection, but implicitly tightens risk appetite.
Gate-4
保证金使用率
Margin Utilization
通过 --margin-used 传入当前使用率（%）；≥ 55% 时禁止新开仓。未传入时由 Flex EOD 数据估算；仍缺失则跳过并标注需人工确认。
Passed via --margin-used (%); ≥ 55% blocks new positions. If absent, estimated from Flex EOD data; still missing → gate skipped and flagged for manual confirmation.
Gate-5
三因子综合评分
Three-Factor Composite Score
VIX 区间（0-35分）+ SP500 位置（0-30分）+ 期限结构（0-25分）→ 总分 < 50 否决。Gate score 写入 m.gate_score 供 AI 解读。
VIX range (0-35 pts) + SP500 position (0-30 pts) + term structure (0-25 pts) → total < 50 rejected. Gate score written to m.gate_score for AI reference.

05
可自动执行的特殊规则
Auto-Executed Special Rules
在标准过滤之外针对特定标的的额外规则
Per-ticker rules beyond the standard filter pipeline

gld_vix_gate

VIX < 22 时禁止开 GLD 的 Put；VIX > 22 或地缘事件触发才允许。VIX < 18 绝对禁止。

Put on GLD blocked when VIX < 22; allowed only when VIX > 22 or geo-event triggered. VIX < 18 is absolute prohibition.

btc_above_ma50

COIN / HOOD 等加密标的要求 BTC 收盘价在 50 日均线上方，否则阻断信号输出。

Crypto-linked tickers (COIN / HOOD etc.) require BTC closing above its 50-day MA, otherwise signal blocked.

btc_drop_8_block

BTC 当日跌幅超 8% 时立即阻断 HOOD 等加密相关标的，防止在极端行情下开仓。

BTC single-day drop >8% immediately blocks HOOD and crypto-adjacent tickers to avoid opening in panic conditions.

require_above_ma200

TSLA 等标的要求股价在 200 日均线上方；跌破则阻断信号，降低趋势做空风险。

Tickers like TSLA require price above 200-day MA. Break below blocks the signal to reduce shorting into a downtrend.

oi_min_5000

CRCL / HOOD 等新标的要求候选合约中至少一个 OI ≥ 5000，确保流动性充足。

New tickers (CRCL / HOOD etc.) require at least one candidate contract with OI ≥ 5,000 to ensure adequate liquidity.

crypto_bucket_cap_4pct

IBIT / COIN / MSTR / HOOD / CRCL / MARA 合计名义暴露不超过账户净值 4%。

IBIT / COIN / MSTR / HOOD / CRCL / MARA combined notional exposure capped at 4% of account NLV.

◈
AI 硬约束（不可越界）：AI 不推荐清单外标的 · 不建议放宽 VIX 35 / 加密池 4% / Put 名义 150% / 单标 10% / 行业 30% 等硬上限 · 不给具体张数 · gates_ok=false 时不建议任何新开仓 · 不编造数值。 AI Hard Constraints (non-negotiable): AI cannot recommend off-list tickers · cannot suggest relaxing VIX 35 / crypto 4% / put notional 150% / single ticker 10% / sector 30% · cannot specify lot sizes · cannot suggest new positions when gates_ok=false · cannot fabricate values.
